Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-11-07
  Features:
    - Biter nests are two houses with tiled roofs and chimneys, and spitter nests are a block of flats or a hall, on paved lots. People still walk out of them.
    - Windows and street lamps in the settlements light up at night.
    - Destroyed settlements leave burnt-out ruins: broken walls, rubble, pieces of fallen roof and a standing chimney on scorched paving.
    - Worms are defensive positions, each manned by a gunner dressed like the spitter of the same tier: an earth-bank pit (small), a concrete pillbox (medium), a concrete bunker with a gun mount on the roof (big) and a steel-plated bunker (behemoth).
    - Gunners stand at ease until enemies come near, then raise their guns and turn to fire. The pillbox's gunner fires from its slit.
    - Destroyed positions leave their wreckage and their dead gunner, in their own shade of clothing colour.
    - The gallery scenario shows the settlements, positions and ruins, and checks that every position still fires and that destroyed nests and worms leave ruins.
  Changes:
    - Nests and worms no longer pulse, screech or roar, and hitting them throws stone chips instead of blood.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.1
Date: 2026-11-06
  Features:
    - Added a "Gore" startup setting. Turning it off stops flesh being thrown when enemies die; the blood stays.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-06
  Features:
    - Blood and gore from wounded and killed enemies is red.
    - Every fallen person gets their own shade of their group's clothing colour.
    - Enemy icons show the engineer, badged with the tier's armor and, for spitters, a gun.
    - Enemies make footsteps instead of skittering, biters' attacks sound like pickaxe strikes, and spitters fire with a gunshot.
    - Removed the bug screeches and calls. People die without a scream.
  Changes:
    - Body sizes stay within 20% of the engineer: 0.85, 0.95, 1.05 and 1.15.
    - Behemoths wear darkened power armor so they stand apart from big enemies without needing extra size.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-06
  Features:
    - Biters and spitters are drawn as people, built from the engineer's own sprites.
    - Each tier is told apart by body size, clothing colour, and armor (none, heavy, power).
    - Biters swing a pickaxe when they attack. Spitters aim a gun, and their acid leaves from the gun barrel.
    - Biter and spitter corpses are fallen people, and their blood is red.
    - Added a "gallery" scenario that lines up every enemy type next to the engineer.
